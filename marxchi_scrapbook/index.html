SITE NOTE

Privacy, in plain English.

Camera: Marxchi Studio asks for camera permission only after you press Start camera.

Photos: This project edits photos in your browser. It does not include an upload feature or an account system. Your images stay in the page until you download them or close/refresh the tab.

Cookies: This site does not use cookies or tracking scripts. It does not collect any personal data.

Feedback: If you send feedback via email, your message will be sent to the email address you provide. It is not stored or shared by this site.

Disclaimer: This is a personal project. It is not affiliated with any company or brand. It is provided as-is, without warranty.
